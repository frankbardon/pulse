## Matrix slot — `MAT_SEM` ordinal two-step statistics (U59 E4)

The first stage of the DWLS / WLSMV estimators: lavaan's two-step sample statistics (`muthen1984` → `lav_samp_step1` / `lav_samp_step2`, what `lavCor(..., ordered = ...)` reports). `internal/sem`: `normal.go`, `bvn.go`, `ordinal.go`, `ordinal_scores.go`, `ordinal_gamma.go`, `wls.go`; the MAT_SEM wiring is the last bullet.

- **What it computes.** `sem.TwoStep(TwoStepInput)` per group (`TwoStepGroups` splits rows by a group index with SHARED `Levels`): an ordered column's thresholds `qnorm(cumulative weighted proportions)` (the exact univariate ML; AS 241 Φ⁻¹), a numeric column's mean and ML variance (divisor Σw), then every pair in lavaan's order (column-major below the diagonal, `Pairs[k]` = lavaan's k-th correlation): the ML polychoric (ordered × ordered, cell probabilities from the bivariate-normal CDF, floored at `eps^(2/3)`), the two-step ML polyserial (numeric × ordered, conditional probability floored at `eps`, ±100 sentinels at the open thresholds) and Pearson (numeric × numeric, closed form). Rows are complete (listwise; the caller drops incomplete rows); weights are FREQUENCY weights (w copies; `TestPolychoric_WeightsAreReplication`).
- **lavaan conventions kept, deliberately.** Polychoric search box ±0.999, polyserial ±0.995 — a maximum outside is reported ON the edge with `Bound` (lavaan's nlminb box). The 2 × 2 shortcuts of `lav_bvord_cor_twostep_fit`: two empty cells → ±1, proportional margins → 0, both thresholds 0 → `−cos(2π·n₁₁/N)` (`Closed`), one empty cell → `zero.add` 0.5 with margins kept; a larger table's empty cells are left alone (`ZeroCells` flags any). The estimate is the exact root of the score (safeguarded Newton, bracketed), NOT lavaan's nlminb stop: lavaan sits up to ~1.3e-7 off the root on the brand fixture.
- **Refusals** are typed `*sem.OrdinalError{Var, Group, Reason, Freq}`: `constant` (an ordered column with one category or all weight on one code; a numeric column without variance), `empty_category` (a declared category holding no weight — the lavaan refusal "some categories are empty in group g", reached when the pooled categories are missing from one group; lavaan's default `allow.empty.cell = FALSE`), `no_data`, `invalid_input`. The engine maps them to `PULSE_*` codes when it wires the estimator in.
- **Casewise scores (Gamma's raw material).** `Scores(row, out)` is lavaan's SC row (layout `Layout()`: thresholds / numeric means, numeric variances, correlations); `PairScores(k, row)` the pair's ρ-, threshold-, mean- and variance-scores (`lav_m84_pair_sc`). All UNWEIGHTED — a frequency-weighted caller multiplies by w (lavaan's INNER for frequency weights is Σ w·s·sᵀ).
- **Gamma** (`ordinal_gamma.go`, `TwoStepStats.Gamma(in)` → `*Gamma`): lavaan's `muthen1984` with its default `numeric_bread = "observed"` — INNER = Σ w·s·sᵀ; A11 per variable (an ordered column's INNER block, a numeric column's observed information Σw/σ², Σw/2σ⁴); A21 / A22 (ordered × ordered: Σ w·ρ-score·threshold-score and Σ w·ρ-score²; a pair touching a numeric column: the observed second derivatives, `lav_m84_pair_obs_deriv`); WLS.W = B⁻¹·INNER·B⁻ᵀ, then H (the cor → cov map of a pair touching a numeric column) and the numeric-mean row / column negated. `NACOV()` = N·WLS.W; `WLSObs()` is the matching statistic vector (thresholds, −mean, numeric variances, off-diagonal covariances). An A11 block or A22 entry that does not invert, or a non-finite figure, is `ReasonSingular`. Held to lavaan's dumps (`gamma` block of the ungrouped fixtures) within 1e-9 at lavaan's estimates, WLS.W on the correlation scale |Δ| ≤ tol·√(W_ii·W_jj) (a pair pinned at ±0.999 beside an empty cell has a variance near 1e10): `TestWLSMV_GammaMatchesLavaan`; digests `TestWLSMV_GammaGoldenBits`.
- **The estimator** (`wls.go`, `sem.FitOrdinal(m, in, est, opt)`, `m` built with `ModelOptions.Ordered`): lavaan's DWLS / WLSMV, delta parameterization, single group. An ordered variable must be an output (no outgoing arrow); its intercept is fixed 0 and its residual variance is no parameter (the remainder 1 − (ΛCΨCᵀΛᵀ)ᵢᵢ, reported fixed); an observed exogenous variable (lavaan's `conditional.x`), `x ~ 1` or `x ~~ x` on an ordered x are `*OrdinalSpecError`. F = Σ v_k (s_k − σ_k)², v = 1/diag(NACOV), σ = WLS.est (τ − μ, a numeric −μ, numeric variances, off-diagonal covariances); the free vector is the model's free parameters then the thresholds (`x | tk` rows, after the table). SEs are lavaan 0.7's robust.sem on categorical data: bread = the OBSERVED information (Richardson-differenced gradient −(N−1)/N·ΔᵀV(s−σ), `information.bread = "observed"`), not ΔᵀVΔ — the expected bread is ~2 % off; vcov = H⁻¹(ΔᵀVΓVΔ)H⁻¹/(N−1); `:=` rows use it. T = (N−1)·F (`objective` = 2·fmin = (N−1)/N·F); its p is null (lavaan's NA). WLSMV adds the scaled-shifted test from the EXPECTED E = ΔᵀVΔ: U = V − VΔE⁻¹ΔᵀV, a = √(df/tr((UΓ)²)), stat a·T + df − a·tr(UΓ); the baseline is the independence model (F_b = Σ_pairs v s², its U = V on the pairs). GFI / AGFI are `gfi_lisrel` / `agfi_lisrel` with W = V (nel = |WLS.obs|); loglik / AIC / BIC NaN. Held to `fit_lavaan_wlsmv.R` dumps (WLS.obs, WLS.V, Γ, WLS.est, Δ, vcov, traces, every row and fit measure) within 1e-5 on estimates and 1e-4 on fit measures: `TestWLSMV_MatchesLavaan`. The fit runs gonum (Fisher scoring) — tolerance-only across architectures, like every lavaan-convention fit; Γ stays bit-exact.
- **MAT_SEM wiring (E4-S2).** `params.estimator` (`ml` default | `dwls` | `wlsmv`, `vectors.SEMEstimators`) and `params.ordered` (labels, `semList`) decode in `vectors.decodeSEMOrdinal`: an ordinal estimator needs a non-empty, duplicate-free `ordered`, ML refuses one, and `solver` ≠ `fisher`, `information`, `singular`, `meanstructure: false`, `group`, `resample` are `bad_params` (E4-S3 lifts the last two); `sem.ModelOptions.Ordered` forces lavaan conventions + the mean structure, and its `*sem.OrdinalSpecError` maps to `bad_params` (`details.param`, `variable`, `problem`). `resolveSEMOrdinal` refuses a standardizing vector and records `OrderedLevels` (packed_bool 2, u4 16, else unknown — vector members are never categorical). `types.MatrixSpec.bufferedParams` buffers `dwls` / `wlsmv` (predict ≡ runtime, `TestPredict_Streamable_MatchesRuntime`). `processing.finalizeSEMOrdinal` codes each ordered member by its sorted distinct values over the slot's listwise rows (frequency weights passed through), runs `sem.FitOrdinal`, and maps a `*sem.OrdinalError`: `constant` / `singular` → `PULSE_MATRIX_SINGULAR`, `empty_category` / `no_data` → `PULSE_MATRIX_INSUFFICIENT_N`, `invalid_input` → `PROCESSING_INTERNAL`, with `details.reason` / `variable`; < 2 rows nulls every figure. Scalars: `semScalarKeys` (chi2_p_value / loglik / aic / bic null) plus, under `wlsmv`, `vectors.SEMOrdinalScalarKeys`. Predict: estimates rows add `SEMPlan.ThresholdRowsEstimate` (schema bound − 1, else 4 per member); p-values add `ThresholdRowsBound` (basis `dictionary` when every bound is known, else `lower_bound` at 1 per member). A Compose SEM fit over an ordinal battery is `PULSE_FIT_INVALID` `sem_ordinal` (`model.SEMOrdinalRefusal`; scoring is E4-S4). Feature `capability:sem_ordinal` (→ `MAT_SEM`, a params surface; prose tokens `semProseTokens`). Gates: `TestMatrixSEM_Ordinal*` (`internal/service`: every lavaan fixture end to end, frequency weight = expansion, worker bits, refusals), `TestSEM_Ordinal*` (root: predict p-values / streamability, refusal parity, Compose), `TestSEM_Hidden*`, `TestCheckFits`. Skill `skills/sem-ordinal.md`.
- **Bits.** The five files (`ordinal_gamma.go` joins the four) are FMA-free (Gamma also calls `linalg.InverseSPD`, a reference kernel) (`float64(a*b)` on every product, a division by a power of two barriered too, since the compiler turns it into a multiply) and call only `internal/detmath` (`Exp`, `Log`, `Sin`, `Cos`, `Asin`) and `math.Sqrt`, so every figure is identical on amd64 and arm64: `TestPolychoric_GoldenBits` (scalar pins + SHA-256 digests of five fixtures' statistics and scores), `TestPolychoric_NoFusibleProducts` (source scan), `TestPolychoric_NoFMAContraction` (arm64 cross-compile, `-d=fmahash=vy`, filtered to the five files).
- **Oracles** (`internal/sem/testdata`, regenerated by `gen_lavaan.sh`'s two-step block; CI never runs R). `bvn_reference.json` (`bvn_reference.R`): 2240 points, tails to |h| = 8, ρ = ±1 and either side of the port's branch edges, each with R's `pbivnorm` (Genz's MVBVU, lavaan's routine) and an independent adaptive quadrature of Sheppard's integral — held to 1e-12 absolute (`TestPolychoric_BVNMatchesReference`; both agree to 2.3e-16). `*_lavaan.json` (`fit_lavaan_polychoric.R`, lavaan 0.7.3, version recorded): the brand survey (nine ratings + recommend; ungrouped, per wave) and the edge sets from `gen_polychoric_data.R` (`poly_extreme` correlations near ±1 and extreme thresholds, `poly_sparse` empty cells, `poly_binary` each 2 × 2 shortcut, `poly_weighted` frequency weights replicated for lavaan). Each carries lavaan's figures (held to 1e-6, `TestPolychoric_MatchesLavaan`) and an independent re-derivation (root-found Sheppard / Olsson scores; held to 1e-12) plus lavaan's SC rows, INNER and A21, compared at lavaan's own estimates within 1e-9 (`TestPolychoric_ScoresMatchLavaan`).
