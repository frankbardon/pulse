# U12 weighted-inference statistics review

The committed record of the statistical review of Pulse's weighted inference (roadmap [U12](../units/U12-weighting-inferential.md)). It is the input for the human statistics reviewer at [U33](../units/U33-v1-release.md) (#206), beside the [U08 review record](U08-statistics-review.md): what was checked, how, what was found, what the docs story fixed and what is still open. U12 changed engine formulas, not guidance metadata, so it has its own file rather than a section of the U08 record.

- **Scope:** the `n_eff` semantics (N* = Σw under `frequency`, Kish n_eff = (Σw)²/Σw² under `probability`; w* = w·N*/Σw; every weighted formula is the frequency formula on w*) and every formula U12 lifted — the moment and count tests (E1), the frequency-only rank / exact / distribution tests (E2), the weighted-host overlays (E3), weighted OLS / penalised OLS / Bayes linear / GLM and the regression attributes (E4), the CI aggregators, z / t scores and quantile buckets (E5) — plus the refusal reasons and the contract prose in `.claude/reference/weighting.md`.
- **Not in scope:** the U11 descriptive aggregators (pinned by U11's reference fixtures), guidance prose (the U08 / U09 gates and record).

## Method

### Deterministic layers (binding CI gates)

1. **Unity parity.** All-ones weights marshal byte-identically to the unweighted run for every weighted operator × execution arm × weight source (`TestWeightUnityParity`, `TestWeightOverlayUnityParity`, the `tests` / `regressions` / `groupers` subtrees; the operation-order rule keeps w ≡ 1 the unweighted recurrence bit for bit).
2. **Frequency expansion.** Integer frequency weights equal the physically duplicated rows run unweighted (`TestWeightFrequencyExpansionParity`, `TestWeightOverlayFrequencyExpansionParity`, …), per (operator, kind) pair the manifest's `weight_kinds` advertises — `assertWeightKindCoverage` fails a pair without a row.
3. **Probability scale invariance.** Probability weights × c answer the same figures (`TestWeightProbabilityQuantileScaleInvariance`, `TestWeightOverlayProbabilityScaleInvariance`, the `probability` arms of the parity gates).
4. **External references.** `TestWeightReferenceValues` pins every weighted figure to values generated offline: stock R 4.6.1 on the `rep()` expansion for `frequency` (plus scikit-learn 1.7.2 for penalised β, Hmisc 5.3.0 for quantiles); the w* closed form, cross-checked with statsmodels 0.14.5 / scipy 1.16.2, for `probability` — and the closed form must equal R on the frequency column first. `TestWeightReferenceKindsAgree` holds point estimates equal across kinds and p-values different. Oracle policy: `.claude/reference/weighting.md` (Reference fixtures).
5. **Predict = runtime.** Every refusal, `n_source` rule and components-disabled refusal is raised by one resolver path shared by predict and runtime (`TestWeight_*MatchesPredict`).

## Verdicts by area

**n_eff semantics.** The core is correct: `KishNEff`, `Basis.NStar` and `Scale` in `inference.go` are right. `Welford.Add` and `ScaledVariance` give c·M2/(N*−1) = n_eff·M2/(Σw(n_eff−1)). That reduces to m2/(n−1) at w ≡ 1 and to the expansion under integer frequency weights. The kish variance in the weighted pairwise twin, m2/(Σw−Σw²/Σw)/n_eff, is algebraically the same quantity. The contract does not claim to be design-based: the "What it is and is not" paragraph and the "not Rao-Scott" labels are present. Two problems remain. The Kish assumption (weights unrelated to the outcome and to the comparison) is never stated (WS-03). One refusal reason wrongly says that under probability weights Σw always overstates the sample size (WS-07).

**Moment tests.** These are correct: one-sample and paired t (df = N*−1), Welch t and z on N*_g, Welch ANOVA (v_j = N*_j/s_j², df on N*_j−1), and Pearson (r is kind-free, t on N*−2, Fisher CI on N*−3). Each equals the expansion under frequency weights and reduces correctly at w ≡ 1. One real defect: `TEST_ANOVA_F` under probability weights uses ONE whole-sample Kish scale. When weights differ between groups this distorts each group's effective size, and it disagrees with every other split test (WS-01). Minor: Pearson can emit NaN t/p when 1 ≤ n_eff ≤ 2, with only a warning (WS-12).

**Proportion z and χ².** Both are correct under frequency weights (`chisq.test(correct=FALSE)` and `prop.test` on the expansion). The p̂_g, N*_g, pooled-rate and Wald-CI algebra is consistent. Cramér's V and φ are scale-free as claimed. Under probability weights, the table-level Kish scaling is disclosed as "not Rao-Scott". The docs do not say when it fails or in which direction (WS-02). The justification for having no low-n_eff floor is vacuous (WS-13).

**Rank and exact tests.** I checked the mechanics and found them correct for the expansion:
- weighted mid-ranks;
- tie sizes taken from the expansion;
- Kendall's pair mass w_i·w_j, with tied-both pairs (copies of one row) excluded and the tau-b denominator and tie-corrected Var(S) on Σw;
- Spearman df = Σw−2;
- Fisher on the Σw table;
- the weighted ECDF step for KS;
- the frequency weighted median for Brown-Forsythe.

The defects are in the stated reasons and caveats:
- The claim that no probability-weighted rank test exists is false: `survey::svyranktest` exists (WS-08).
- The Shapiro-Francia refusal reason contradicts the expansion argument used for the rank tests (WS-09).
- The KS asymptotic p is applied to an expansion full of ties, with no warning (WS-10).
- The Kendall reference uses `continuity = TRUE`, which the doc does not say (WS-11).

**Regressions and GLM.** Residual df = N*−p−1, σ̂² = c·RSS/df and SEs on w* are correct. The doc correctly says this is NOT `lm(weights=)`, whose df is the row count; the misconception the brief warned about is absent. The GLM uses prior weights w*, Cov = (XᵀW*X)⁻¹ and null μ = weighted ȳ, which matches R `glm(weights = w*)` with dispersion 1. Penalty scaled by Σw matches glmnet's weight normalisation. The leverage formula equals diag W½X(XᵀWX)⁻¹XᵀW½. One defect: when n_eff < p+1 the residual df goes negative and the code clamps σ² to 0. That reports standard errors of 0, a residual standard error of 0 and an adjusted R² above 1, where the honest answer is undefined (WS-05). Leverage under frequency weights is the sum over a row's copies, which matters for standard cutoffs (WS-14). The probability-arm "references" check arithmetic only; they do not validate the method (WS-04).

**CI aggregators.** No findings. The bounds are mean ∓ z·√(c·M2/((N*−1)·N*)). At w ≡ 1 this is the unweighted z interval. Under integer frequency weights it equals R `mean ∓ qnorm·sd/√n` on the expansion. The merge carries N and Σw².

**Scores and quantile buckets.** No findings. The population sd √(M2_w/Σw) is scale-free and equals `DescrStatsW(ddof=0)`. The bucket rule ⌊⌊C−1⌋·k/W⌋ is consistent with the documented order statistic r = ⌈b·W/k⌉: being in bucket b ⇔ C ≥ ⌈bW/k⌉+1. The normwt rescale and the integer snap match Hmisc in exact arithmetic.

**Overlays.** Correct:
- `readMeanLeg` / `ScaledVariance` follow the one-formula rule.
- The union n_eff (ΣΣw)²/Σ(Σw²/n_eff) is correct for disjoint legs.
- χ² row, column and matrix scaling mirrors `TEST_CHISQ`.
- Proportion bases read N*_base.
- `NSourceRefusal` correctly refuses raw and distinct counts.

The defect is an inconsistency. `OVERLAY_PAIRWISE_WEIGHTED_TWO_MEANS_Z` is classed "both kinds" and accepts `n_basis: "weights"` under a probability weight, which reads Σw as the sample size. The engine refuses exactly that for `cell_weight_sum` (WS-06).

## Open items for the U33 human reviewer (U12)

- **WS-05 / WS-12: undefined figures reported as numbers.** OLS with N* ≤ p + 1 clamps σ² to 0 (SEs 0, adjusted R² > 1); Pearson with n_eff ≤ 2 reports null t / p behind only a warning. Decide: NaN (`null` on the wire) or a refusal.
- **WS-06: `OVERLAY_PAIRWISE_WEIGHTED_TWO_MEANS_Z` `n_basis: "weights"` under probability weights** reads Σw as n — what `NSourceRefusal` refuses for every other pairwise kind. Its computation is the shipped v0.39.1 contract, which U12 deliberately did not change; decide whether to refuse or force `kish` on a probability host.
- **WS-10:** weighted `TEST_KS` p on a tie-heavy expansion is conservative with no ties warning.
- **WS-13:** `TEST_PROP_Z` has no scaled expected-count guard on small N*_g.
- **WS-07 / WS-08 / WS-09: refusal and classification reasons.** The prose behind three decisions is overstated (Σw "overstates" — it is scale-arbitrary; "no probability-weighted rank test exists" — `survey::svyranktest` does, but needs design information Pulse lacks; Shapiro-Wilk "defined only for unweighted samples" — the frequency expansion argument applies). The decisions stand; the reason strings should be reworded.
- **The probability arm has no independent oracle** (WS-04): no software implements Kish-w* inference, so the probability references confirm Pulse's arithmetic, not the method. The method's justification is the one-rule decision (`.claude/reference/weighting.md`, Weighted inference — What it is and is not), which this reviewer signs off or not.
- **Kish's assumption** (WS-03, WS-02): n_eff is the variance inflation of a weighted MEAN for weights unrelated to the outcome; Pulse applies it to slopes, correlations, contrasts and tables as a heuristic. Confirm the documented framing (screening and description; design-based tools when the design matters) is acceptable for v1.

## Panel independence

One pass of one model family; treat "no finding" (CI aggregators, scores, quantile buckets) as weak evidence. The deterministic layers above are the stronger guarantee for `frequency` (exact expansion against stock R); for `probability` they guarantee internal consistency and scale invariance only.
